¡Hola Barcelona!

A side-scrolling game about the long way home — three stops across Barcelona, each level drawn from living here.

Role Design & Code
Year 2026
Type Browser Game
Built with Vibe coding, Canvas
01

Overview

¡Hola Barcelona! is a browser game built from my own experience living in Barcelona. You play as Yemmy, taking the long way home across three stops.

Vibe coded — no engine, no framework. Coins, three lives and a different hazard on every stop.

← → to walk, hold X to run, Z or Space to jump — stomp to squash. Enter starts and pauses, Shift mutes, and 1 2 3 jump between stops. The pad and buttons on the console work too.  Open it full screen ↗

02

Three stops home

Eixample — balconies and pickpockets.

Barceloneta — sand, gulls and jellyfish.

Metro L5 — commuters and pigeons.

03

Making it

Every sprite, the skyline, the HUD and the travel card are drawn frame by frame onto one canvas.

The console shell is pure CSS and scales to any screen.
