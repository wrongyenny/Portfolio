Insights.

Role UX/UI Designer
Year 2025
Client Weecover
Tools Figma, Illustrator
Type Web App · Insurtech

Insights. is an insurance management platform built for Weecover, an insurtech.

It replaces PDF forms, spreadsheets and email chains with one interface for the whole policy lifecycle, from quotation to emission.

A 5-step wizard keeps the long quotation flow focused, one decision per step.
